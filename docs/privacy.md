# Privacy Policy — Campus Classroom

*Last updated: 2026-10-20*

Campus Classroom is a private Google Classroom add-on for Nanyang Junior
College (NYJC). It connects the school's Campus platform to Google Classroom
so teachers can attach Campus assignments to coursework and students can
complete them inside Classroom.

## What we process

- **Identity.** When you connect your Google account, we verify that its
  email address matches your Campus account email. The match result is
  logged; no Google password or credential is ever seen by this app.
- **Google Classroom data.** Via the Classroom API and only within the
  scopes you grant (courses, rosters, coursework, student submissions —
  read-only, plus the add-on attachment scopes), we read course and
  coursework context to link Campus assignments to Classroom, and to show
  the right assignment to the right student.
- **Assignment content.** Questions, responses, and feedback live in the
  Campus platform, not in this app. This app is an interface layer and
  stores no assignment data locally.
- **Credentials.** This app never stores Google OAuth tokens — not in a
  database, files, or sessions. Access tokens are obtained on demand from
  the Campus token broker and held in memory only for the lifetime of a
  request.
- **Usage telemetry.** Assignment attempt and completion events are
  recorded in the Campus platform to help teachers support their students.

## Who processes it

Data is processed by the Campus platform operated by NYJC Computing for
school instructional use, under the school's data governance and
Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

## Draft tester opt-out

If you are listed as a draft tester for this application and wish to opt
out, email **nyjc.computing@nyjc.edu.sg** and your address will be removed
from the tester list.
