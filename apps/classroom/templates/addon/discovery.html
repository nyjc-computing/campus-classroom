Assignments are posted from Campus Classroom

This add-on doesn't create assignments inside Google Classroom. Post them from the platform and they appear in your classes automatically.

  1. Open Campus Classroom in a new tab (the button below).
  2. Choose an assignment and select Send to Google Classroom.
  3. Pick your classes — the assignment posts to Classroom with its interactive attachment attached.
