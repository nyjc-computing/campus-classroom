Campus Classroom

An assignment platform that integrates seamlessly with Google Classroom. Create structured questions, collect student responses, and provide feedback—all within the Classroom experience.

Google Classroom Add-On
Teachers create assignments, students respond within Classroom.

Key Features

Structured Assignments

Create assignments with hierarchical questions—main questions, parts, and subparts.

Google Classroom Integration

Students work directly within Google Classroom. No separate login or interface.

Teacher Feedback

Provide targeted feedback on each question response. Grades managed in Classroom.

How It Works

1
Teacher Creates Assignment

Build structured questions with prompts and answers on the Campus Classroom platform.

2
Link to Google Classroom

Send the assignment to one or more Google Classroom classes with a click.

3
Students Complete in Classroom

Students open and submit assignments without leaving Google Classroom.

4
Teacher Provides Feedback

Review responses and give targeted feedback. Assign grades in Classroom.
